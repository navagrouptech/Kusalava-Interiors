Climate Resilience

Built For The Andhra Pradesh Climate

High humidity, coastal salt air, and intense summers require specific material engineering.

100% Calibrated BWP Marine Ply

Resists coastal humidity and condensation. Zero delamination, zero fungal growth, and zero sagging across large cabinet spans.

Anti-Corrosive Framing

Heavy-duty galvanized steel sections and SS 304 hardware prevent rust and salt-air oxidation in coastal cities like Vizag and Kakinada.

Thermal & Acoustic UPVC

Double-glazed acoustic windows keep coastal winds, monsoon downpours, and highway noise outside while reducing summer AC cooling loads.

Washable Luxury Emulsions

Teflon-protected, anti-fungal luxury paint formulations that endure high-temperature humidity without color fading or chalking.

Andhra Pradesh FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you travel to cities across Andhra Pradesh for on-site measurements?

Yes. Founders Pavan Kumar and Surya personally visit your property in Vijayawada, Visakhapatnam, Guntur, Rajahmundry, or any of the 27 confirmed AP cities for laser site surveys and layout consultations.

Where are modular carcasses manufactured for AP projects?

Precision cutting and PUR edge-banding are performed in our controlled manufacturing facility. Modular flat-packs are carefully transported and assembled on-site by our trained master carpenters to ensure zero transit damage.

How are payment stages handled for outstation projects in AP?

Payments follow a transparent four-stage milestone schedule: 10% design booking, 40% design sign-off and woodwork procurement, 40% modular carcass assembly and ceiling framing on site, and 10% final handover after joint snag inspection.
