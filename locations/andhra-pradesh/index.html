Climate Resilience

Built For The Andhra Pradesh Climate

High humidity, coastal salt air, and intense summers require specific material engineering.

100% Calibrated BWP Marine Ply

Resists coastal humidity and condensation. Zero delamination, zero fungal growth, and zero sagging across large cabinet spans.

Anti-Corrosive Framing

Heavy-duty galvanized steel sections and SS 304 hardware prevent rust and salt-air oxidation in coastal cities like Vizag and Kakinada.

Thermal & Acoustic UPVC

Double-glazed acoustic windows keep coastal winds, monsoon downpours, and highway noise outside while reducing summer AC cooling loads.

Washable Luxury Emulsions

Teflon-protected, anti-fungal luxury paint formulations that endure high-temperature humidity without color fading or chalking.

Andhra Pradesh FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you travel to cities like Vijayawada, Guntur, and Vizag for site measurements?

Yes. Kusalava Interiors regularly travels across Andhra Pradesh for on-site surveys, client meetings, and project supervision. Pavan Kumar and Surya coordinate site schedules directly with you.

Where are the modular wardrobes and cabinets manufactured?

Precision cutting and PUR edge-banding are performed in our controlled facility, and modular flat-packs are carefully transported and assembled on-site by our trained master carpenters to ensure zero transit damage.

How do you handle project payments and milestones?

We work with transparent, milestone-linked payments tied to verifiable stages: design sign-off, material procurement, carcass installation, and final handover after a joint inspection.
