Vijayawada FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the cost of turnkey home interior design in Vijayawada?

Turnkey interiors for a 3 BHK apartment or bungalow in Vijayawada typically range between ₹10.5 Lakhs and ₹22 Lakhs. For independent villas spanning 3,000+ sq.ft, budgets range from ₹25 Lakhs upwards depending on imported veneer finishes, pooja room brass detailing, and false ceiling coves.

How is on-site execution managed in Vijayawada?

Founders Pavan Kumar and Surya oversee initial on-site laser measurements and weekly milestone checks. Precision modular carcasses are manufactured in our workshop and installed on-site by our master carpenters under direct supervision.
