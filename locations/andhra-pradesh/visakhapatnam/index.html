Vizag FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is commercial plywood unsuitable for homes in Visakhapatnam?

Commercial MR (Moisture Resistant) plywood uses urea-formaldehyde resins that disintegrate when subjected to continuous coastal humidity and sea breeze, resulting in wood swelling, peeling laminate, and mold. Kusalava Interiors strictly uses 100% calibrated IS:710 Boiling Water Proof (BWP) marine plywood with phenol-formaldehyde bonding for complete durability.

What is the typical cost for a 3 BHK interior in Vizag?

Complete turnkey interior execution for a 3 BHK apartment in areas like MVP Colony, Beach Road, or Madhurawada typically ranges between ₹11 Lakhs and ₹22 Lakhs, including anti-corrosive hardware, BWP marine ply modular wardrobes, false ceilings, and painting.
