Hyderabad FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer on-site consultations across Hyderabad?

Yes. Founders Pavan Kumar and Surya personally visit your property in Jubilee Hills, Gachibowli, Kokapet, or any area in Hyderabad to conduct laser site surveys and understand your aesthetic aspirations.

What is the typical turnaround time for a 3 BHK apartment in Hyderabad?

A complete turnkey interior for a standard 3 BHK apartment-including modular wardrobes, false ceilings, painting, electrical, and kitchen-typically takes between 40 to 55 working days from drawing sign-off to handover.

Are your modular cabinets made from marine plywood?

Yes, we strictly use 100% calibrated IS:710 Boiling Water Proof (BWP) marine plywood for all modular carcasses to withstand Hyderabad's monsoon humidity and ensure decades of structural integrity.
