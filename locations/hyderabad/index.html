Hyderabad FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct, verified answers regarding home interior design in Hyderabad and Secunderabad.

What is the average cost of interior design for a 3 BHK in Hyderabad?

A turnkey 3 BHK interior in Hyderabad typically costs between ₹11 Lakhs and ₹22 Lakhs depending on material selections. Basic modular joinery with commercial ply and 0.8mm laminate begins around ₹8 Lakhs, while calibrated IS:710 BWP marine plywood, German Blum hardware, acrylic finishes, and architectural false ceilings range from ₹14 Lakhs to ₹22 Lakhs.

How long does turnkey interior execution take for a flat in Hyderabad?

Turnkey execution for an apartment in Hyderabad requires 30 to 45 working days following 2D/3D design approval. Standalone villas and extensive duplexes typically take 55 to 70 working days. We adhere strictly to milestone timelines because all carpentry, false ceiling, and electrical teams are in-house.

Do you service newly handed-over gated communities in Kokapet, Tellapur, and Narsingi?

Yes. We actively execute turnkey projects in newly delivered gated communities across Kokapet, Tellapur, Narsingi, Gachibowli, and Financial District. Our leadership coordinates directly with community facility management teams to comply with work-hour regulations and material entry guidelines.

Can I hire Kusalava Interiors for modular cupboards or false ceilings only in Hyderabad?

Yes. In addition to full turnkey residences, we undertake individual craft services including custom bedroom wardrobes, living room false ceilings, acoustic glass partitions, and specialized wall painting across Hyderabad.
