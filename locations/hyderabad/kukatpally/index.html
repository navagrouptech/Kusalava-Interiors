Kukatpally FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the budget for a 2 BHK or 3 BHK flat interior in Kukatpally or Miyapur?

For a 2 BHK apartment in Kukatpally or Miyapur, complete turnkey interiors typically range from ₹7.5 Lakhs to ₹13 Lakhs. For a 3 BHK, costs range from ₹10.5 Lakhs to ₹18 Lakhs. This covers modular kitchen, wardrobes, false ceilings, lighting, and painting with calibrated marine ply.

Do you use commercial particleboard or genuine marine plywood?

We strictly reject particleboard and MDF for core carcasses. Kusalava Interiors exclusively builds with calibrated IS:710 Boiling Water Proof (BWP) and IS:303 BWR marine plywood, ensuring water resistance and complete termite immunity.
