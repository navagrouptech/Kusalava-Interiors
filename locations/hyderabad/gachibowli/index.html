Gachibowli FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the turnkey interior cost for a 3 BHK in Gachibowli or Kokapet?

Turnkey interiors for a 3 BHK in Gachibowli or Kokapet typically range from ₹12 Lakhs to ₹24 Lakhs. This includes calibrated BWP 710 marine ply carcasses, acrylic or PU finished shutters, designer false ceilings with magnetic tracks, a bespoke modular kitchen with quartz countertop, and luxury wall paint.

How do you manage interior work in gated communities with strict noise rules?

We strictly adhere to residential community association rules: noisy cutting and drilling is restricted to authorized weekday hours (typically 10 AM to 5 PM). We minimize site noise by carrying out precision modular cutting and edge-banding in our controlled workshop, delivering flat-packs for silent on-site assembly.
