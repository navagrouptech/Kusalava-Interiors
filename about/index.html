Materials & Rigor

Uncompromising Material
Specifications.

We never compromise on base materials. Our builds are engineered for multi-decade durability against moisture, humidity, thermal expansion, and daily family use.

BWP 710 Marine Plywood

All modular carcasses, wardrobes, and kitchen structures utilize calibrated Boiling Water Proof (BWP) grade plywood, ensuring absolute defense against warping and termite damage.

Laser-Calibrated Ceilings

False ceilings are constructed with Saint-Gobain Gyproc plasterboards and heavy-gauge galvanized steel framing, laser-aligned for zero hairline cracks and perfectly true shadow gaps.

German Soft-Close Hardware

Drawer runners, concealed hinges, and lift-up systems feature certified German hardware (Blum/Hettich compatible), tested for over 100,000 continuous opening cycles.

Certified MEP Coordination

Electrical and plumbing conduits are pressure-tested and concealed with detailed circuit distribution diagrams, ensuring safety, quiet operation, and maintenance ease.
