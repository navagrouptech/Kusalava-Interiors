LIVING ROOM & RECEPTION

Living Room Interior Design Ideas: Spatial Planning, Fluted Paneling & Statement Lighting

By Kusalava Interiors Architectural Team • Published 2026-02-28 • 6 min read

From grand double-height villas in Jubilee Hills to panoramic high-rise penthouses in Financial District, the modern living room is designed around human conversation, curated sightlines, and tactile surfaces.

1. Creating Conversational Furniture Islands

Avoid pushing all sofas flat against the perimeter walls. Instead, float a low-profile sectional or paired armchairs on a large hand-tufted wool or silk rug to define an intimate conversational island.

Ensure at least 36 inches of clear circulation space around all seating clusters for effortless movement during family gatherings and celebrations.

2. Bookmatched Marble Feature Walls

A full-height TV or fireplace accent wall crafted from bookmatched Italian marble-such as Statuario, Calacatta Gold, or Grey William-serves as an organic, one-of-a-kind art piece.

Pair marble slabs with vertical fluted oak slats and warm backlighting to soften stone acoustic reflections and add organic warmth.

3. Disguising the Entertainment Technology

Modern living room design prioritizes aesthetics over exposed black screens and tangled power wires. Consider flush-mounted Frame TVs that display fine art when inactive, combined with acoustically transparent fabric panels concealing high-fidelity soundbars.
