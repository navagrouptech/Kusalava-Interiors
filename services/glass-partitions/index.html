Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our Glass Partitions process in Hyderabad & Andhra Pradesh.

Is toughened glass safe for homes with children?

Yes. All our glass partitions use heat-strengthened toughened safety glass which is 5 times stronger than regular annealed glass. In the unlikely event of breakage, it crumbles into small granular pebbles rather than sharp shards.

How does acoustic glass keep conversations private in home offices?

Acoustic partitions utilize specialized acoustic PVB interlayers sandwiched between glass sheets, which absorb sound energy and suppress speech frequencies.

Can fluted or reeded glass be used in bathroom shower cubicles?

Yes. Fluted glass creates elegant privacy while allowing light through, and we treat the smooth side with hydrophobic nano-sealant to prevent water and limescale stains.
