Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our Cupboard Works process in Hyderabad & Andhra Pradesh.

What plywood grade is used for Kusalava Interiors cupboards?

We use calibrated IS:710 Boiling Water Proof (BWP) marine plywood for all structural carcasses. Unlike commercial MDF or particleboard, marine ply resists moisture, humidity, and bending even in coastal Andhra Pradesh and humid monsoons.

Can cupboards be customized to specific bedroom ceiling heights?

Yes. Every cupboard is 100% custom-fabricated to your exact ceiling height, eliminating unsightly dust-trap gaps at the top and maximizing vertical storage efficiency.

How long does a typical wardrobe project take to complete?

From 3D design approval to final on-site installation, a typical custom wardrobe suite is completed within 20 to 30 days.
