Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our Home Automation & Smart Works process in Hyderabad & Andhra Pradesh.

Can smart home automation be installed during interior renovation or only in new builds?

We implement smart automation seamlessly in both ongoing interior renovations and new constructions, utilizing concealed neutral wiring conduits and wireless Zigbee/Matter protocols.

Do your motorized curtain tracks support heavy blackout drapery and double tracks?

Yes. Our high-torque motors easily pull up to 60kg of heavy velvet or acoustic blackout fabrics on dual tracks (sheer + blackout) with near-silent operation.

Will smart switches work if our home internet is temporarily disconnected?

Yes. All switches retain full physical manual touch operation, and smart local gateway automations continue operating locally even without an active internet connection.
