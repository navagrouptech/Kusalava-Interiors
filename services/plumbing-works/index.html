Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our Plumbing Works process in Hyderabad & Andhra Pradesh.

How do you guarantee that concealed pipes will not leak behind tiles?

Before any wall or floor tile is laid, our team conducts a rigorous 24-hour hydrostatic pressure test at 10 Bar (twice normal domestic water pressure) to certify 100% leak-proof joints.

What is the benefit of a concealed cistern over a floor-mounted toilet?

A concealed cistern hides the water tank inside the wall cavity, saving valuable bathroom floor space, elevating aesthetics, and allowing seamless floor cleaning underneath the WC.

Can you install multi-jet rain showers in high-rise apartments?

Yes. We evaluate line water pressure and, when necessary, install silent in-line booster pumps to guarantee invigorating water volume at every shower fixture.
