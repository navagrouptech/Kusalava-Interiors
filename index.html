About Kusalava Interiors

Complete Interior Solutions,
Executed With Mastery.

"We believe interior architecture should inspire quiet confidence — where every joint is seamless, every shadow gap is intentional, and every texture invites touch."

Kusalava Interiors is a turnkey interior design, build and decoration firm founded on the principle that exceptional living spaces require both artistic vision and rigorous engineering discipline. Rather than relying on disconnected sub-contractors, we provide comprehensive in-house solutions spanning bespoke modular cabinetry, architectural false ceilings, luxury wall treatments, precision aluminium profiles, acoustic glass partitions, and certified MEP works.

Architectural Design & 3D Planning

Meticulous 2D spatial layouts, realistic 3D renderings, and ergonomic lighting designs tailored to your family's daily rhythms.

In-House Construction & Structural Joinery

Commercial-grade marine plywood, German hardware fittings, laser-leveled false ceilings, and certified structural glazing.

Artisanal Finishing & Dedicated Handover

Venetian plaster textures, seamless cove lighting transitions, zero-snag delivery, and full long-term craftsmanship support.

Our Craftsmanship

Explore our eight specialized interior disciplines. Each service is executed by trained master builders using premium materials and uncompromising quality standards.

01 / CARPENTRY

Cupboard Works

Bespoke modular wardrobes, walk-in closets, dresser units, and TV consoles engineered with BWR marine ply, fluted laminates, and soft-close German hardware.

02 / ARCHITECTURE

False Ceiling Works

Architectural gypsum and POP suspended ceilings, indirect warm cove lighting troughs, recessed magnetic track lights, and laser-straight perimeter shadow gaps.

03 / FINISHES

Painting Works

Artisanal Italian Venetian plaster, velvet matte luxury emulsions, textured concrete feature accents, and micro-sanded flawless wall preparation.

04 / METALS

Aluminium Works

Architectural matte black anodized framing, slim glass facade systems, decorative exterior louvers, pergolas, and heavy-duty sliding track doors.

05 / GLAZING

Glass Partitions

Fluted, reeded, tinted, and clear acoustic toughened glass dividers with slimline metal grids for luxury walk-ins, ensuites, and modern study rooms.

06 / ELECTRICAL

Electrical Works

Architectural lighting automation, concealed fire-retardant conduits, designer brass toggle switches, phase load balancing, and magnetic track channels.

07 / PLUMBING

Plumbing Works

High-end sanitary installations, concealed thermostatic diverters, floating vanity plumbing, rain shower heads, and pressure-tested CPVC pipe networks.

08 / FENESTRATION

UPVC Windows

High-performance multi-chamber UPVC windows and panoramic sliding doors featuring double-glazed acoustic glass, thermal insulation, and multi-point locks.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our turnkey interior design and execution in Hyderabad & Andhra Pradesh.

What interior design and execution services does Kusalava Interiors provide?

Kusalava Interiors provides complete turnkey interior solutions including custom cupboard works and modular wardrobes, false ceilings, luxury wall painting, slim-line aluminium works, acoustic glass partitions, certified electrical works, luxury sanitary plumbing, and UPVC window systems across Hyderabad and Andhra Pradesh.

Which areas in Hyderabad and Andhra Pradesh do you serve?

In Hyderabad, we serve Jubilee Hills, Banjara Hills, Gachibowli, Financial District, Hitec City, Madhapur, Kokapet, Kondapur, Begumpet, and surrounding regions. In Andhra Pradesh, we provide turnkey services in Vijayawada, Guntur, Visakhapatnam, Rajahmundry, Eluru, Kakinada, and Tirupati.

Do you provide turnkey interior solutions or only individual services?

We provide both complete turnkey end-to-end residential and commercial interior solutions as well as specialized individual craft services like cupboard fabrication, false ceilings, or glass partitions based on client requirements.
